Clean Verbatim

It only deletes.

Ums, stutters, restarts and anything you take back with “scratch that” disappear. Clean Verbatim never rewords, reorders or adds a word. When a word could be real, it stays.

Scored blind on 160 examples set aside while it was built: 0 lost a real word. How it was measured ↗

Where words go

Words go where they belong.

In Mail, Notes and Slack, say “New paragraph.” In a terminal, end with “Send it.” and it presses Return. Terminals never get a line break you did not ask for.

Printed on your Mac

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Speech recognition runs on your Mac. OpenRamble goes online only to download the speech model when you click, and to check for updates.
